The Three Fatal Flaws of Speech Adjudication

Why traditional evaluation fails and how VOCODE's physics-grounded approach solves it.

⚠️

1. Unilateral Penalization Bias

Rule-based speech tools evaluate metrics in isolation. An intentional 2.5s pregnant pause before a climax is flagged as dead air, and intimate quiet delivery is penalized as mumbling.

VOCODE: Systemic Impairment Coupling (Ψ)
📉

2. Subjective Feedback Gap

Human judges provide vague impressionistic notes ("felt flat", "lackluster presence") lacking exact millisecond timestamps and actionable physical vocal mechanics.

VOCODE: rhetorical Moves & Physical Drills
🕳️

3. The ASR Corpus Void

Corpora like CommonVoice and LibriSpeech evaluate what words were spoken (WER), completely ignoring the acoustic physics of how they were delivered.

VOCODE: 4-Tier Paired Contrastive Dataset

End-to-End System Pipeline

From heterogeneous raw audio to broadcast standardization, 100 Hz physical telemetry, and dual LLM reasoning.

Stage 1

Ephemeral Ingestion

4-Layer Monologue Gatekeeper validates turn-taking and SNR. Temporary audio is purged post-inference maintaining 0 MB disk footprint.

Stage 2

Broadcast Standardization

16 kHz sinc resampling, 70 Hz Butterworth HPF eliminating handling rumble, and -20.0 LUFS EBU R128 loudness normalization.

Stage 3

100 Hz Physical Telemetry

Praat Parselmouth micro-prosodic pitch dynamic range (semitones), PAPR crest factor (dB), and the 20% Oratorical Pause Law.

Stage 4

Non-Unilateral Rubric

Evaluates 5 Oratorical Pillars (10.0 pts) coupled via Systemic Impairment (Ψ_impair) and a dynamic Leniency slider (λ ∈ [0, 100]).

Stage 5

Move Classification

Partitions speech phrases into rhetorical cards: Brilliant (cyan), Great (green), Inaccuracy (yellow), Mistake, and Blunder (red).

Stage 6

Dual-Layer LLM Reasoner

Deterministic DSP proofs + Groq Qwen 27B / Llama 3 semantic audience impact explanations and 4 daily practice routines.
